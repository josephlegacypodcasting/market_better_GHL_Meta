The B2B Webinar Sponsorship Evaluation Checklist

12 Questions to Ask
Before You Sponsor a Webinar

A media-buyer's guide to evaluating any B2B webinar or content platform with clarity, before you commit budget.

B2B sponsorship spend has grown quickly, and not every "opt-in audience" is created equal. Two platforms promising the same reach can differ enormously in who actually shows up, how honestly your brand is positioned, and how much real data you get back after the show. This checklist gives you twelve questions to bring into any sponsorship conversation, so you can evaluate a platform with clarity before signing a contract, and it draws directly on Market Better's own standards as a webinar platform built for marketing and revenue leaders.

Bring these twelve questions to any sponsorship conversation. A platform worth partnering with will welcome every one of them.

01 Audience Quality & Reach

1

Is registration genuinely opt-in, and can they prove attendees are your actual target buyers?

Reach numbers mean little if the audience isn't right. A reputable platform will document how registrants opt in and show you the audience's role, seniority, and industry mix before you commit.

2

What is the average and live attendance rate — not just the registration count?

A large registration list with a low show-up rate is a vanity metric. Ask for the actual live attendance rate across recent shows, not a one-time best-case example.

3

What percentage of the audience matches your ideal customer profile by company size and revenue?

Ask for a firmographic breakdown — company size, revenue band, and seniority — so you can confirm the audience actually overlaps with your buying committee before you invest.

02 Positioning & Content

4

Will your brand be positioned as the solution, or just a logo on a slide?

The strongest sponsorships build the show's narrative around the problem your product solves. Ask specifically how your brand is worked into the content, not just where your logo appears.

5

Who writes and controls the content, and do you have input into the narrative?

Know upfront whether the platform will collaborate with you on the show's angle, or whether you're buying into a fixed format with no say in the story being told.

6

What does audience engagement actually look like during the show?

Ask about host-read mentions, branded polls, live Q&A, and real-time interaction. Passive attendance is a very different experience from an audience actively engaging with your brand.

03 Lead Delivery & Sales Enablement

7

What data do you receive after the show, and how quickly?

Ask exactly what lands in your inbox — a full lead dashboard with real-time access, or a delayed spreadsheet with limited fields.

8

Do you get raw contact details, or only aggregate analytics?

Aggregate charts are useful for reporting, but your sales team needs names, titles, companies, and engagement signals to actually follow up.

9

Is there a sales enablement kit you can put to work in outreach?

Ask whether the full recording, branded short-form clips, and other assets are included, and whether you're free to use them across social and sales outreach after the show.

04 Investment & Terms

10

Is pricing transparent and tied to clearly defined deliverables?

You should receive a clear breakdown of what each sponsorship tier includes before you sign, not a verbal figure that shifts once you're in a contract.

11

What's included at each tier, and what could add cost later?

Ask directly whether email promotion, co-editorial input, or guest placement are part of the package, or whether they're billed separately as the partnership grows.

12

Can you start with a single webinar, or are you locked into a long-term contract?

A platform confident in its results will let you prove the model with one show before asking for a bigger commitment. High-pressure, long-term-only terms are a signal to slow down.
